Read SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md and catalog/methods.json relative to this repository.
Define the question, representation, assumptions, success criterion, certificate,
budget and stop rule. Retrieve methods matching the question; do not run every
method merely because it is cataloged. Separate candidate generators, search
operators, prerequisites, obstacles and independent validators.

Prefer the configured axiomweave-research-systems MCP when task-scoped and enabled.
Runtime: scripts/research_mcp.py. Its 21 tools are bounded controls, not a universal
solver. Catalog-only means unavailable. If MCP is off, inspect the repository's
typed contracts and documented scripts; do not claim tool discovery occurred.

Apriori is the intended symbolic-combination method, unrelated to financial
trading schemas. Require proved hereditary rejection before pruning extensions.
Retain cancellation pairs, exhaustive/random baselines, domain checks and exact
certificates. Beam failures are evidence of incompleteness.

Use AxiomWeave's ledger for material assumptions/questions/decisions with evidence,
C0-C6 ceiling, falsifier, cost/risk budget and stop rule. Candidate search, high
scores and multiple assistants agreeing do not certify theorem correspondence.
No base-model weight changes, live trading, cloud actions, messages or broad
process termination are implied by this skill.

Loop within a frozen finite grammar and cost. Stop after three non-improving
rounds, budget exhaustion or a failed negative control; final-sweep the known
universe and report unexplored space. Seed saturation never proves all future
methods have been discovered. Use separate held-out tasks before utility promotion.
Keep local MCP profiles default-off and close only the exact temporary child.
